### Revolut Bundles ChatGPT Go Into Premium Banking
**Source:** [FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/07/31/revolut-teams-up-with-openai-to-offer-chatgpt-go/) - **Date:** July 31, 2026

Revolut partnered with OpenAI to give eligible retail customers access to ChatGPT Go at no additional cost for up to 12 months, depending on their plan. FinTech Global reported that Ultra and Metal customers receive 12 months, Premium, Revolut Pro, and Revolut Mobile customers receive six months, and Plus and Standard customers can claim three promotional months. The offer adds higher usage allowances, expanded file uploads, document analysis, image generation, and memory features to Revolut's membership bundle.

This is not just a perk story. Revolut is treating general-purpose AI access as part of the premium banking value proposition, alongside travel, work, health, and lifestyle services. Banks should be blunt about what this signals: customer relationships are expanding beyond balances and payments into daily digital utility. The risk is that institutions bundle AI without clear data-use boundaries, customer support readiness, or a reason the offer reinforces the financial relationship instead of becoming a subsidized subscription.

### Chime Cuts 10% of Workforce in an AI Operating Pivot
**Source:** [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/banking/2026/chime-cuts-10percent-of-workforce-in-ai-pivot/) - **Date:** July 31, 2026

PYMNTS reported that Chime plans to lay off about 150 people, roughly 10% of its workforce, citing a Bloomberg report and an internal memo from CEO Chris Britt. Britt said the company needs new skills to harness AI, smaller teams, and more operating discipline as a public company. Chime also said about 70% of support interactions now run through voicebots and chatbots, with chatbots resolving about 75% of those conversations and voicebots resolving 66% of calls where members choose self-service. The company has also cited AI-assisted coding as a driver of internal product velocity.

Financial-services leaders should not pretend this is only a cost-cutting headline. It is a workforce redesign signal. AI changes the mix of customer-service, engineering, risk, and operations roles. The hard work is making sure automation gains do not hide service failures, unfair treatment, brittle escalation paths, or a weaker control environment. Boards should ask which processes are being redesigned, which roles are disappearing, what quality metrics are improving, and where human judgment remains mandatory.

### Zywave Expands Apex AI for Insurance Broker Growth

Zywave expanded its Apex AI platform with Summer 2026 capabilities for insurance brokers, carriers, and agents. The release adds tools across prospecting, renewals, quoting, and client management, including an expanded Producer Agent with Microsoft Outlook integration, targeted prospect outreach, scheduling support, renewal-preparation automation, and recommendations based on enriched broker and agency data. The insurance signal is clear: AI is moving from claims and underwriting into distribution productivity and renewal management. That can help producers spend less time on administration, but it can also create sloppy targeting, weak consent practices, and poor documentation if firms treat automated outreach as harmless. Brokerages should lock down approved messaging, data sources, supervision, producer accountability, and evidence that renewal recommendations were suitable for the client.

### Cantina Launches Agentic Remediation for Security Backlogs
**Source:** [FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/07/31/cantina-exits-stealth-with-8m-to-fix-securitys-backlog/) - **Date:** July 31, 2026

Cantina emerged from stealth with USD 8 million in new capital, bringing total funding to USD 16.5 million. The company is targeting the gap between faster AI-enabled vulnerability discovery and slower human remediation. FinTech Global reported that Cantina's platform models customer environments across systems such as Okta users, servers, GitHub repositories, AWS IAM roles, and databases containing customer PII, then uses agents to automate discovery, investigation, coordination, remediation, and verified resolution.

For banks and FinTechs, this is the security version of the AI productivity problem: finding more issues is useless if remediation capacity does not move. AI security platforms should be judged on risk prioritization, ownership mapping, change evidence, verification, and auditability, not alert volume. The biggest adoption trap is buying another dashboard while leaving engineering, compliance, and risk teams with the same slow handoffs.

### Okta Buys Permiso to Secure AI and Machine Identities
**Source:** [Fintech News Singapore](https://fintechnews.sg/135194/security/okta-permiso/) - **Date:** July 31, 2026

Okta agreed to acquire Permiso Security to strengthen identity threat detection and response across human, machine, and AI identities. Fintech News Singapore reported that Permiso analyzes more than 2,500 security signals across more than 70 identity technology partners, including unusual activity, unused permissions, policy violations, and risky AI agent behavior. The deal is expected to add monitoring and containment for compromised or misconfigured AI agents in real time.

This is directly relevant to financial institutions because agentic AI turns identity security into a production risk function. A bank cannot rely on login controls if the dangerous actor is a service account, a workflow bot, or an AI agent with excessive permissions. Identity teams need inventories of non-human actors, scoped privileges, behavior analytics, kill switches, and incident playbooks that assume agents can act faster than people can review.
