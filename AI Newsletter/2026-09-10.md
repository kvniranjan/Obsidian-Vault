### Visa, Mastercard, and Ant International Develop an Interoperable Know-Your-Agent Framework

**Source:** [Ant International, Mastercard, and Visa via PR Newswire APAC](https://en.prnasia.com/releases/apac/ant-international-mastercard-and-visa-initiate-collaboration-on-know-your-agent-interoperability-to-scale-agentic-commerce-547272.shtml) - **Date:** September 10, 2026

Ant International, Mastercard, and Visa began work on a Know-Your-Agent interoperability framework for card networks, digital wallets, agent platforms, and marketplaces. The proposed framework would let participants recognize common trust signals while retaining their own verification and decisioning processes. The collaboration focuses on linking each agent to a validated operator, cardholder, or business; shared security and behavioral certification requirements; and continuous monitoring using identity and transaction signals. The work will run through BuildFin.ai, the Monetary Authority of Singapore-convened platform, and build on the SAFR framework for agentic finance at runtime.

This is an architecture signal, not a finished standard. For banks, the core problem is becoming attribution and authority: who controls the agent, what was it allowed to do, and can the institution revoke or investigate that authority after the fact? Interoperability could reduce repeated onboarding, but it could also create false confidence if trust signals, liability, privacy, dispute handling, and revocation rules remain vague. Risk and payments teams should engage early, while treating any eventual KYA registry or certification as an external dependency that needs independent testing.

### PayNearMe Acquires Marr Labs to Embed Agentic AI in Payment Operations

**Source:** [PayNearMe](https://home.paynearme.com/press/paynearme-acquires-marr-labs-to-accelerate-ai-innovation-across-the-end-to-end-payment-experience/) - **Date:** September 9, 2026

PayNearMe acquired Marr Labs' technology assets and hired key employees to accelerate agentic AI across its Payment Experience Management platform. PayNearMe says it processes more than $50 billion in payments annually for more than 20,000 businesses. Marr Labs built voice, messaging, and workflow automation for regulated environments, including technology that supported millions of customer interactions each month across financial-services companies. Terms of the transaction were not disclosed.

The strategic point is that payment AI is moving closer to the whole journey, from a payment request through support, exceptions, and reconciliation, rather than staying in a separate chatbot. That creates a more useful operating surface but also a larger blast radius. Lenders, credit unions, and payment providers should define which actions an agent can recommend, which it can execute, and which require a human or dual approval. They should also test call and message accuracy, consent, complaint handling, payment reversals, audit trails, and failure recovery before treating the acquisition as production evidence.

### Mastercard and Backbase Link AI, Data, and Cyber Capabilities for 120-Plus Banks

**Source:** [Backbase](https://www.backbase.com/press/mastercard-and-backbase-announce-strategic-collaboration-to-accelerate-ai-native-banking-transformation) - **Date:** September 9, 2026

Mastercard and Backbase announced a collaboration that makes Mastercard capabilities available alongside the Backbase AI-native Banking Operating System. The capabilities include Dynamic Yield for personalization, Test & Learn for measuring product and pricing changes, SpendingPulse for macroeconomic and sector-level insights, and Cyber Quant for cyber-risk assessment. Backbase says more than 120 banks use its platform across retail, small-business, commercial, private, and wealth banking. Both companies position the collaboration as a path from AI experimentation to governed execution within defined customer consent parameters.

The important development is the operating-layer distribution. If a platform used by many banks becomes the place where customer context, analytics, cyber signals, and agents meet, its permissions, change controls, and outage behavior become part of the bank's risk perimeter. Banks should demand clear data-use boundaries, evidence that personalization does not create unfair steering, independent testing of customer outcomes, and resilience plans for shared platform dependencies. The announcement shows commercial reach and integration intent, not measured agentic banking outcomes.

### Sigma360 and Spheros Connect AI Risk Intelligence to Commercial Onboarding

**Source:** [Sigma360](https://www.sigma360.com/sigma360-and-spheros-partner-to-connect-secure-business-data-sharing-with-risk-intelligence/) - **Date:** September 9, 2026

Sigma360 and Spheros announced a partnership that connects secure B2B data exchange with AI-powered risk intelligence. Spheros will integrate Sigma360 through an API so organizations can screen companies, beneficial owners, and connected parties during onboarding and ongoing due diligence. The combined workflow covers sanctions and watchlist screening, adverse media intelligence, entity resolution, and monitoring, with a target market of banks, fintechs, and other financial institutions. Spheros says its reusable data layer helps financial institutions onboard commercial clients up to 80% faster, a vendor-reported claim.

This addresses a real weakness in commercial onboarding: collecting information and understanding relationship risk often happen in separate queues. Faster intake is useful only if the risk decision remains defensible. Compliance leaders should require source-level provenance for adverse media, explainable entity matches, consent and data-retention controls, documented thresholds for escalation, and tests for missed connected parties. Continuous monitoring also needs clear ownership so an alert changes a customer risk record rather than simply adding another queue for analysts.

### Automation Anywhere Makes Agentic Procure-to-Pay Generally Available

**Source:** [Automation Anywhere via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/automation-anywheres-new-agentic-procure-to-pay-solution-extends-autonomous-finance-across-the-procurement-lifecycle-302873084.html) - **Date:** September 9, 2026

Automation Anywhere announced general availability of an agentic Procure-to-Pay solution for the Office of the CFO. The system connects procurement, ERP, finance, and supplier systems across vendor onboarding, requisitions, purchase orders, goods receipt, invoice processing, and payment-status tracking without replacing existing systems of record. When exceptions occur, an AI assistant surfaces context and recommends actions. Automation Anywhere says its wider Autonomous Finance suite includes more than 55 AI agents across more than 100 task types and 10 finance sub-functions, while its invoice engine has delivered up to 90% straight-through processing for customers. Those impact figures are company-reported.

For banks and insurers, internal finance operations are a lower-risk place to learn agentic execution than customer credit or payment authorization, but they still touch cash, suppliers, accounting, and segregation of duties. The useful control boundary is to automate routine coordination while keeping exceptions and money release under explicit approval. Buyers should test duplicate invoices, unauthorized vendor changes, tax and accounting treatment, reconciliation, approval routing, evidence retention, and what happens when the agent or an upstream ERP is unavailable.
