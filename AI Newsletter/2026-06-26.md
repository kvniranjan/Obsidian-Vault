### Backbase Buys Kasisto to Push Banking From Chatbots to Agentic Resolution

Backbase acquired Kasisto, bringing Kasisto's agentic AI platform, financial-services intelligence, and New York team into the Backbase AI-native Banking OS. Backbase argues that many bank AI deployments still answer questions without resolving work, leaving customer intent split across channels, contact centers, and operations.

The blunt read: banks are outgrowing chatbot theater. If an agent can only summarize balances or route a customer to a form, it will not change cost or service outcomes. Customer-facing agents need permissions, limits, escalation rules, and failure monitoring before they touch account actions.

GrailPay raised a $10.5 million Series A led by MissionOG to expand its Payments Identity Network for B2B payments. The company frames the network as a trust layer for bank-account risk as instant settlement and agentic commerce remove the time and reversibility buffers that traditional payment workflows relied on.

This is the control problem behind faster B2B money movement. When payments become instant and software agents can initiate or route transactions, banks and payment processors need stronger account-level identity, authorization, and risk signals before money moves. Waiting for downstream returns, disputes, or manual reconciliation is too late. The winner in agentic payments may be the institution that knows when not to send.
