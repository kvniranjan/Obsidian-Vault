### ECB Links AI Adoption to Systemic Cyber and Concentration Risk

**Source:** [ECB Financial Stability Review, November 2026](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/financial-stability-publications/fsr/html/ecb.fsr202611~e03b919d36.en.html) | **Date:** November 18, 2026

The European Central Bank's November Financial Stability Review says banks and asset managers are increasingly using AI for credit scoring, fraud detection, and portfolio management. It warns that highly capable and agentic AI can increase the speed and scale of attacks, introduce new failure modes, and leave euro area banks dependent on concentrated third-party providers. The ECB says those dependencies can change how cyber incidents propagate and create a risk of systemic disruption. The review was endorsed by the ECB Governing Council.

The board-level issue is concentration, not just model error. Banks should map which critical services share cloud, model, data, or API providers, then test what happens when a provider fails, changes a model, restricts access, or suffers a cyber incident. AI risk reporting that omits shared upstream dependencies is incomplete.

### Danamon Explores AI Agents for Collections and Telesales

**Source:** [Danamon and Advance Intelligence Group strategic AI partnership](https://www.danamon.co.id/en/news/2026/11/24/Danamon-dan-Advance-Intelligence-Group-Menandatangani-Kemitraan-Strategis-di-Bidang-AI) | **Date:** November 24, 2026

Bank Danamon Indonesia and Advance Intelligence Group signed an MOU to explore two AI-agent solutions for collections and telesales. The proposed agents would support customer engagement through voice and text, drawing on AIGL's consumer-finance, credit, identity, risk, and agentic-AI capabilities. Danamon says its teams will provide governance and oversight, and customer data will be processed under Indonesian data-protection and data-localisation requirements. Possible work in foreign exchange and card products remains exploratory.

This is an MOU, not production evidence. Collections and telesales are high-risk customer-facing uses where an apparently helpful conversation can become coercive, misleading, or unsuitable. Before deployment, Danamon should define contact permissions, language and vulnerability controls, escalation to a human, call-record retention, complaint handling, and how the bank will prove that the agent stayed within approved scripts and authority.

### Mirae Asset Puts Evidence-Linked AI Search Into Employee Workflows

**Source:** [Mirae Asset Securities Product Knowledge AI Search via AWS](https://press.aboutamazon.com/aws/2026/11/mirae-asset-securities-makes-accessing-product-information-faster-and-more-trustworthy-with-aws) | **Date:** November 20, 2026

Mirae Asset Securities says its Product Knowledge AI Search is now in full use across employee workflows. An ontology and knowledge graph link financial product data to about 13,000 disclosure and research documents. Multiple AI agents divide retrieval and answer composition, provide the source and as-of date, and state when the requested information is unavailable. The service passed Korea's Financial Security Institute generative-AI security assessment after security reviews, red teaming, and performance re-validation. Mirae and AWS report that more than 450 employees ran over 2,000 complex queries, with answer quality on 250 business questions about 84% above its March pre-launch validation. These usage and quality figures are company and vendor reported.

Source-linked answers are more useful than a generic assistant, but a citation does not prove that an answer is current, complete, suitable, or appropriate for a customer. Financial institutions should preserve retrieval lineage, stale-data handling, model-change revalidation, entitlement checks, and the exact version of information shown when an employee or customer acts on an answer.

### NEOPAY Adds AI Fraud Intelligence to Middle East Acquiring

**Source:** [ACI Worldwide and NEOPAY fraud-management expansion](https://investor.aciworldwide.com/news-releases/news-release-details/aci-worldwide-expands-strategic-partnership-neopay-adding-ai) | **Date:** November 24, 2026

UAE-based merchant acquirer NEOPAY is expanding its relationship with ACI Worldwide and will deploy ACI Fraud Management across its acquiring ecosystem. ACI describes a combination of machine learning, behavioral analytics, predictive risk scoring, and real-time decisioning for card-present and card-not-present fraud. ACI says its intelligence draws on billions of transactions and produces decisions in milliseconds. These are vendor and customer announcement claims; no measured fraud-loss, approval-rate, or false-decline result was disclosed.

Putting adaptive fraud controls at the acquirer can improve coverage across merchants, but it also concentrates consequential decisions in a shared service. NEOPAY should measure false declines by merchant and customer segment, document explainability and appeal paths, test cross-border data controls, and make sure feedback from disputed transactions does not quietly reinforce biased or self-confirming rules.

### Shinhan Publishes Banking-Specific Tests for LLMs and Agents

**Source:** [Shinhan Bank's ICAIF 2026 research on K-BankEval and Agent Foundry](https://shinhangroup.com/kr/archive/press/detail/1040) | **Date:** November 23, 2026

Shinhan Bank presented research on two controls for financial AI. K-BankEval contains 5,000 questions across six areas: banking expertise, retrieval and evidence-based answers, tool calling, banking calculations and reasoning, consumer-protection safety, and agent task planning. Shinhan says its comparison of six LLMs found that strong general-purpose performance did not guarantee strong performance on banking tasks. Its Agent Foundry platform also evaluates the final answer and the execution path, including tool-use records, requirements compliance, and process efficiency. These are bank-reported research results, not an independent benchmark or production certification.

The useful idea is to test what an agent did, not only what it said. Banks should require evidence that evaluations cover tool selection, permissions, failed calls, unsafe plans, customer-protection constraints, and escalation. A polished final answer can hide an unacceptable path to reach it.
