### Mambu Connects Core Banking, Payments, and Agentic AI

**Source:** [Mambu](https://mambu.com/en/insights/press/mambu-launches-intelligent-core) - **Date:** September 2, 2026

Mambu launched Intelligent Core, a technology architecture combining Mambu Core, Mambu Payments, and the new Mambu Agentic layer. The company describes a native Model Context Protocol connection to core and payments, an AI Insights layer for trusted data, and pre-built agents that can act within configurable policies, approval thresholds, and business logic while explaining each action and decision.

The strategic pitch is straightforward: banks should not keep bolting AI onto systems that cannot safely expose real-time context or execute controlled actions. The harder question is whether direct access to the ledger makes entitlements, segregation of duties, rollback, change control, and third-party concentration safer or riskier. Buyers should demand proof that permissions hold across every connector and that a failed agent cannot create an unreconciled financial position.

### UOB Puts More Than 300 AI Use Cases Into Production

**Source:** [QA Financial](https://qa-financial.com/uob-puts-testing-behind-300-ai-deployments/) - **Date:** September 1, 2026

United Overseas Bank has moved more than 300 AI and analytics use cases into production, according to an interview with Head of Enterprise AI Alvin Eng. The bank also has more than 30 domain-specific chatbots and Microsoft Copilot available to more than 30,000 employees. Its operating framework includes a central use-case inventory, risk-based controls, pre-deployment validation, monitoring for performance, bias, and drift, plus human oversight, escalation thresholds, and kill switches for higher-risk applications.

This is the useful counterexample to model-chasing. Scale comes from repeatable operating discipline, not from having the most impressive model in a lab. Banks trying to copy the result should require a use-case charter, named business ownership, measurable value attribution, independent validation where needed, and post-release evidence that controls still work after data and workflow conditions change.

### Kyndryl and Incore Bank Test Agentic AI for KYC

**Source:** [Kyndryl and Incore Bank](https://www.kyndryl.com/in/en/about-us/news/2026/08/agentic-ai-incore-bank) - **Date:** August 31, 2026

Kyndryl, Incore Bank, and Google Cloud completed a proof of concept using Gemini models and Kyndryl's Agentic AI Framework to support customer onboarding and ongoing risk assessment. Multiple agents work across structured and unstructured data, internal systems, and trusted external sources to extract and validate information, gather evidence, identify risk factors, produce explainable risk scores, and create auditable records for compliance professionals. The proof of concept achieved up to 99% accuracy in extracting data from onboarding documents and showed potential to reduce onboarding time from months to days.

The distinction matters: 99% extraction accuracy is not 99% correct KYC risk decisions. The result is a promising evidence-gathering workflow, not permission to remove compliance judgment. A production review should test entity resolution, adverse data, missing documents, escalation quality, model drift, and the ability to reconstruct exactly why a risk assessment was produced.

### Cheche Deploys Five AI Agents Across NEV Insurance

**Source:** [Cheche Group](https://ir.chechegroup.com/2026-09-01-Cheche-Group-Launches-ABAO-Agent-Family%2C-Deploying-AI-Across-the-Full-NEV-Insurance-Value-Chain) - **Date:** September 1, 2026

Cheche Group launched the ABAO Agent Family, five specialized AI agents built on its insurance large language model for new-energy-vehicle insurance. The external agents support first notice of loss, damage assessment, claims updates, underwriting, and pricing. Internal agents handle customer-service quality control, settlement follow-up, and non-standard carrier settlement documents. Cheche says the claims platform is live with Volkswagen Anhui and Avatr and integrated with PICC, Ping An, and China Pacific Insurance. It also reports a 30% increase in settlement workflow efficiency and a 50% increase in settlement processing efficiency without additional headcount.

Vertical data is the real differentiator here, not the agent label. Cheche says its pricing model uses more than 200 dynamic risk factors, including vehicle and ADAS or ADS data, across a five-tier segmentation model. Carriers still need independent validation, clear boundaries between support and pricing decisions, claims fairness testing, monitoring for changes in vehicle data, and a defensible process when an agent's recommendation is overridden.

### Candor Brings Cross-Document Validation Before Mortgage Underwriting

**Source:** [Candor Technology](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/01/3354414/0/en/candor-technology-launches-candor-di-to-identify-and-correct-loan-data-discrepancies-before-underwriting.html) - **Date:** September 1, 2026

Candor Technology launched Candor DI, a document intelligence engine that indexes, classifies, extracts, and reconciles mortgage data before a file reaches underwriting. It compares information across pay stubs, tax returns, credit reports, and the Uniform Residential Loan Application, then flags discrepancies with a source-level audit trail. The product can run standalone, through an API, or inside Candor's Loan Engineering System. A Federal Savings Bank executive cited a 15 to 20 minute reduction in manual document indexing and classification per loan file.

This is a more credible AI use case than letting a model write the final credit decision. The value sits in catching inconsistent evidence early and preserving provenance for the underwriter. Mortgage lenders should still set explicit exception thresholds, test extraction across document types and borrower profiles, and separate document-quality findings from credit eligibility so a clean file is not mistaken for a safe loan.
