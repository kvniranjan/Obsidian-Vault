### NatWest Launches Spending Insights Tool and Fraud Triage Agent

**Source:** [The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/natwest-launches-ai-tools-for-spending-and-fraud-support)  •  **Date:** October 1, 2026

NatWest unveiled two AI initiatives in a single wave. The Fraud Triage Agent, integrated into NatWest's Cora chatbot, allows customers to describe suspicious card transactions in plain language rather than selecting from predefined categories; since launch it has processed nearly 19,000 customer conversations, routing each to the appropriate fraud, scam, or dispute team. The simultaneous launch of a proactive financial-coaching tool and a reactive fraud-triage tool from the same bank shows where consumer AI is maturing: both sides of the customer relationship — money management and protection — are now live in production. The 81% figure on human access is the governance constraint every bank should hard-wire into design: AI as a routing and analysis layer, not a substitute for human escalation. The proprietary SLM choice also signals NatWest is managing the data-sovereignty question for UK retail banking rather than defaulting to a third-party frontier model.
