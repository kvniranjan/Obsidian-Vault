### Bank of England and FCA Survey: 86% of UK Financial Firms Now Use AI

**Source:** [Bank of England](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/report/2026/artificial-intelligence-in-uk-financial-services-2026)  •  **Date:** October 26, 2026

The Bank of England and FCA's fourth joint survey of AI in UK financial services, drawing on 196 firms, found that 86% now use AI — up from 75% in 2024 and 58% in 2022. Generative AI accounts for 56% of reported AI systems, sharply up from 17% in 2024, and 80% of respondents have deployed AI agents, though 80% of those agents remain in decision-support roles. Third-party AI now makes up 39% of all systems, and concentration among a small number of cloud and technology providers is the top concern cited by firms. The survey covers participating firms only, not the sector as a whole.

The shift from 17% to 56% generative AI in two years means every compliance, model risk, operational resilience, and consumer-outcomes function at a UK regulated firm now needs a current AI inventory and a live governance process — not a future-state plan. The 39% third-party share and concentration concern directly reinforce the operational resilience expectations under DORA and UK SYSC rules already in effect. The gap between 80% agent deployment and only 2% with broadly autonomous agents also marks where governance has held the line so far and where pressure to expand autonomy will build next.

The round size and investor names are confirmed; customer-count and revenue figures have not been independently disclosed.

The combined strategic investor and customer role of Visa and Capital One is a direct signal that two major U.S. payment networks regard AI-scale attacks as real and scaling faster than legacy detection can match. Financial institutions evaluating shared threat-intelligence services should understand precisely what behavioral and transactional data leaves their environment, how it is anonymized before sharing, who else is in the consortium, what the liability position is if a false positive blocks a legitimate customer, and whether a data-breach at the intelligence provider creates downstream exposure.
