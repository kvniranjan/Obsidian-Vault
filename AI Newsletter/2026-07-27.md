### Flock Launches Agentic AI for Fleet Insurance Risk
**Source:** [The Fintech Times](https://thefintechtimes.com/flock-launches-agentic-ai-assistant-jay-for-uk-fleet-insurance/) - **Date:** July 27, 2026

Flock launched Jay, an agentic AI assistant for UK fleet insurance. The Fintech Times reported that Jay uses more than a billion kilometres of driving data to give fleet managers and brokers real-time risk insight through Flock's portal. The product sits at the intersection of telematics, underwriting, broker service, and ongoing fleet-risk management.

For insurance leaders, this is more than a chatbot attached to a portal. If the assistant can translate driving behavior into actionable risk signals, it can change how fleets manage premiums, safety interventions, broker advice, and renewal discussions. The control questions are obvious: whether the assistant explains the data behind its risk view, whether recommendations affect underwriting or pricing, and how insurers prevent biased or misleading advice when driving data is incomplete.

### FinTech Futures Warns AI Offer Decisions Need Hard Governance
**Source:** [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/ai-in-fintech/before-you-let-ai-make-offer-decisions-ask-these-five-questions) - **Date:** July 27, 2026

FinTech Futures published a governance warning for financial-services firms preparing to let AI make or shape customer offer decisions. The article notes that AI is already helping marketers draft offers, assemble disclosures, and build journeys, while the volume of personalized offers, rates, and disclosures is outpacing manual compliance review. The next step, letting systems act with less human review, is where risk increases quickly.

This matters for banks, lenders, and insurers because offer logic is regulated conduct, not just marketing optimization. AI-generated personalization can create unfair treatment, inconsistent disclosures, suitability issues, fair-lending exposure, and weak evidence when challenged by regulators. Before firms automate offer decisions, they need clear eligibility logic, approved disclosure libraries, adverse-action support where relevant, monitoring by segment, and a record of why each customer saw each offer.

### AegisAI Raises USD 36 Million as AI Spear Phishing Accelerates
**Source:** [FinTech Global](https://fintech.global/2026/07/27/aegisai-lands-36m-to-counter-ai-spear-phishing-surge/) - **Date:** July 27, 2026

FinTech Global reported that AegisAI raised USD 36 million to counter AI-enabled spear phishing. The company's State of the AI Threat in Email study, based on more than 20,000 phishing, scam, and malware emails, found AI-generated spear phishing rose from 2.8% to 13.9% of observed phishing during 2025. That is a fivefold increase in a channel that already drives account takeover, wire fraud, credential compromise, and business email compromise.

Financial institutions should treat this as a fraud and operational-risk issue, not only a security tooling story. AI lowers the cost of personalized attacks against relationship managers, treasury staff, call centers, finance teams, and customers. The practical response is phishing-resistant authentication, stronger payment-change verification, behavioral monitoring, executive impersonation controls, employee reporting loops, and fraud-playbook testing against realistic AI-written lures.
