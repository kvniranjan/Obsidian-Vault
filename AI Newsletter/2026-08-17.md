### TransFi Launches AI Compliance Intelligence for Cross-Border Payments
**Source:** [Global FinTech Series](https://globalfintechseries.com/news/transfi-launches-jarvis-an-ai-powered-compliance-intelligence-platform-for-cross-border-payments/) - **Date:** August 17, 2026

Global FinTech Series reported that TransFi launched JARVIS, an AI-powered compliance intelligence platform for cross-border payments. The platform consolidates customer and transaction data from internal systems and third-party providers, combines KYC, sanctions screening, internet profiling, behavioral and biometric signals, fiat monitoring, and blockchain transaction monitoring, and gives compliance, risk, and operations teams a unified risk view.

The useful signal is the shift from separate compliance tools to a single operating view. Cross-border payment firms cannot scale emerging-market corridors if analysts have to reconcile identity, sanctions, blockchain, behavioral, and fiat risk in different systems. The control question is whether JARVIS improves evidence and escalation quality, not just speed. Compliance leaders should demand explainable recommendations, retained evidence, analyst overrides, and MLRO-visible accountability.

### 360factors Adds Fair-Lending Testing Agent for Banks and Credit Unions
**Source:** [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/360factors-launches-fair-lending-compliance-testing-agent-in-ask-kaia-302851864.html) - **Date:** August 14, 2026

360factors announced a Fair Lending Compliance Testing agent inside Ask Kaia, its AI platform for US banks and credit unions. The agent is designed to help institutions upload LAR data and supporting documentation, select peer institutions, and generate dashboards covering borrower segments, geography, pricing patterns, document coverage, and exam-ready workpapers.

This is where AI in lending compliance gets concrete. Fair-lending risk is rarely visible in one loan file. It appears across patterns, exceptions, pricing, geography, and peer comparison. AI can reduce spreadsheet labor, but it must not turn fair-lending review into a black box. Banks need reproducible datasets, clear peer-selection logic, documented assumptions, human compliance signoff, and workpapers that stand up under examiner challenge.

The article describes consumer-facing travel AI converging with enterprise AI agents that help with hotel-partner follow-up, supplier onboarding, payment routing, authorization optimization, and service triage.
