# eigenfocus

## Verdict
Eigenfocus is worth a product owner's time if the team needs a small, self-hosted planning board without Jira-level ceremony. It is not a full product discovery suite, but it gives a PO enough structure for issue intake, backlog grooming, due dates, labels, comments, board/list views, and delivery follow-through. The catch is simple: the free edition is useful, but team-scale features sit behind the paid edition.

## What It Is
Eigenfocus is a source-available, self-hosted project management and issue-tracking application. The free edition includes unlimited projects, boards, issues, list and board views, Markdown descriptions, attachments, labels, comments, due dates, time tracking, reports, and focus tools. The paid edition adds team-scale features such as roles, custom fields, multiple saved views, grid and timeline views, custom statuses, templates, and SSO.

## Why It Is Useful For Product Owners
For backlog refinement, Eigenfocus can hold raw ideas, bugs, chores, and candidate stories in one place with labels and comments. For prioritization, it gives enough structure to separate now, next, later, blocked, and validation-needed work without forcing a heavyweight methodology. For stakeholder alignment, due dates, comments, and visible boards help make tradeoffs easier to explain. For delivery coordination, list and board views keep status visible, while time tracking can expose whether a team is spending effort on planned product work or operational drag.

## How I Would Actually Use It
1. Create one project per product area and use labels for `customer-request`, `bug`, `tech-debt`, `experiment`, `discovery`, and `stakeholder-ask`.
2. Keep a weekly backlog refinement view where unlabeled issues must be clarified, rejected, merged, or moved into a ready column.
3. Put acceptance criteria in the Markdown description and use comments for stakeholder decisions, not random Slack memory.
4. Use due dates only for real external commitments, such as launch dependencies, compliance dates, or customer promises.
5. Track time for interrupt-driven work for two weeks to show how much capacity is being eaten by support, bugs, or unplanned requests.
6. Use the board view for delivery status and the list view for backlog triage, because those are different PO jobs.

## Limitations / Watch Outs
This is not Productboard, Aha, Jira, or Linear. It will not solve portfolio roadmapping, customer segmentation, feature scoring, or enterprise stakeholder reporting by itself. The free edition is intentionally limited, and the repository says external pull requests are not accepted because there is a paid version. The license is source-available rather than a clean standard open-source license, so do not assume normal OSS freedoms.
