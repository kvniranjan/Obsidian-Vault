This is worth a product owner's time if you need usable starting points for PRDs, experiments, surveys, ROI cases, and strategy reviews. ## What It Is

This is a small Markdown template and framework library, not a product application. It has three parts: reusable document templates, strategic frameworks, and methods for structured product thinking. It includes usage guidance and copy/pull instructions, but no tracker integration, automation service, database, or built-in analytics engine.

## Why It Is Useful For Product Owners

- Backlog refinement: use the product-thesis PRD to force the problem, rationale, scope, success measure, and open questions into a short artifact before creating tickets.
- User stories and requirements: adapt the PRD structure's build and measurement sections into a feature brief, then split only the validated scope into stories.
- Prioritization: use the ROI impact estimate and confidence/assumption sections to expose weak evidence before ranking work.
- Discovery: use the survey design and survey analysis templates to connect questions, findings, hypothesis checks, and next actions.
- Stakeholder alignment: use the strategy kernel to separate diagnosis, guiding policy, and coherent actions in a decision memo.
- Experimentation: use the A/B analysis template to review quality, segments, leading indicators, risks, and a keep-or-kill decision rather than relying on one topline metric.
- Delivery coordination: use the devil's-advocate method before commitment to surface missing dependencies, risks, and confirmation bias.

## How I Would Actually Use It

1. Copy `Product-Thesis-PRD-Template.md` into a feature folder and refuse to schedule refinement until the problem, why-now thesis, evidence, measure, and open questions are filled in.
2. Turn a raw list of feature requests into ROI estimates with baseline, expected impact, confidence, assumptions, and risks before asking engineering for capacity.
3. Draft a four-question customer survey with the survey-design template, including what decision the results will change, then analyze responses with the paired survey-analysis template.
4. Run a pre-roadmap strategy review using the Rumelt kernel: diagnosis, guiding policy, and a short list of coherent actions.
5. Prepare a ship decision from experiment data with topline, segment, quality, leading-indicator, and risk sections, ending in keep, iterate, or kill.
6. Ask a peer or AI assistant to apply `devils-advocate-strategy.md` to a proposed roadmap bet and attach the objections to the decision record.

## Limitations / Watch Outs

- The repository has no visible stars, forks, issues, release history, or topic metadata, so external validation is minimal.
- It has only four commits and the README's last-updated date is 2026-06-17; maintenance may have stopped.
- The accessible page does not show a declared license. Do not copy it into a commercial or redistributed product until licensing is confirmed.
- The material is Claude Code and AI-tool friendly, but the templates are plain Markdown and can be used manually or in Codex with no special integration.
- The templates are starting points, not evidence. A polished PRD or scorecard can still encode bad assumptions.
- The survey templates reference external methods and sources; verify the source claims and adapt them to the audience, privacy rules, and research context.
- There is no connection to Jira, Linear, GitHub Issues, analytics, experimentation platforms, or a roadmap system.
